QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Good questions
deserve clear answers.

Not sure where AI belongs in your business? Start here, then let’s discuss your actual workflow.

What kinds of work can you automate?+

Anything that follows a repeatable pattern: responding to enquiries, moving information between tools, scheduling, reporting, document processing, follow-ups, support triage, and internal notifications. We identify where automation creates the most practical value first.

Do we need to replace our current software?+

Usually not. We build around the tools you already use, connecting platforms such as WhatsApp, Gmail, calendars, CRMs, spreadsheets, forms, and project-management systems.

How long does an automation take to build?+

A focused workflow can be ready within one to two weeks. More connected systems take longer because we allow time for testing, edge cases, team feedback, and a reliable handover.

Will AI replace our team?+

No. The goal is to remove repetitive, low-value work and give people more time for relationships, judgment, and high-impact tasks. Your team stays in control of the workflows and escalation rules.

What happens after launch?+

We provide handover documentation and support. With a Growth System, we also include 30 days of optimisation. Ongoing AI Partner engagements give you a continuous improvement roadmap.
